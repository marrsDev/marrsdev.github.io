Why most home owners choose sliding windows

  1. Easy and simple to operate

    -Aluminium sliding windows are incredibly easy to operate as you only need to move them in a sideways motion. Further, the sliding panels sit on anti-friction roller wheels which makes sliding the window very effortless.

  2. Improved Airflow

    The pependicular orientation of Aluminium slider windows ensures minimal obstruction, providing far better airflow compared to many other window designs.

    The simple sliding mechanism of aluminium slider windows have very few components that can be damaged.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

  • Are aluminium sliding windows and doors secure?

    Very much. The material’s strength makes it difficult to break or force open, and several measures can be taken for added security. These may include:

    • Adding anti-burglar grills
    • Using multi-point locking systems

    Img Source @storyset

  • Can aluminium windows be customized?

    Absolutely! Aluminium windows and doors offer endless customization options. You can choose from a variety of designs and finishes to match your specific taste.

  • How long do they last?

    Aluminium slider windows are known for their longevity. With proper installation, you can expect them to last for decades.

  • Cost

    The price of sliding windows in Kenya and how it compares with traditional ones is a usual question among aspiring homeowners. Interestingly, aluminium sliding window frames cost only marginally more than steel casement designs, while being a lot more affordable than uPVC alternatives. This makes aluminium framing a budget-friendly choice without compromising on modern functionality or aesthetic appeal.

  • What maintenance do aluminium windows and doors require?

    The mechanical parts (Rollers, hinges etc) can last years without needing any kind of repair. They also do not require regular painting or sealing. Simply cleaning your glass window with soap and water keeps it looking good as new. The powder-coated finishes for aluminium profiles are also resistant to scratches and fading, reducing the need for frequent upkeep.

    Image by @Rastudio

  • Can they reduce noise?

    Yes, aluminium windows and doors, especially when paired with double-glazed or laminated glass, offer excellent sound insulation. This makes them perfect for homes and offices located in noisy urban areas or near busy roads.

    Contact one of our technicians here for more information on soundproofing glass windows in Kenya.

  • How do I choose the right type of aluminium windows for my property?

    The best choice depends on your specific needs, such as uses case, ventilation, space, and design preferences. For example, sliding windows and doors are ideal for areas with limited space, while casement windows offer excellent airflow.
